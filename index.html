How it works

Moving out

  1. 1

    One date runs everything. Enter your move-out day. Every task lands in the week it's due.

  2. 2

    Book on day one. Your clean-up booking appears the day you give notice, linked to council's booking page.

  3. 3

    Snap the pile. One photo. AI sorts each item into clean-up, e-waste, donate or repair, and checks it fits the 2 cubic metre limit. You confirm before anything is booked.

  4. 4

    Catch the drop-off days. E-waste, chemical clean-out and repair sessions show up if they fall before you go.

  5. 5

    Short on time? With less than four weeks to go, you get every legal option straight away.

Moving in

  1. 1

    Before you arrive. From the address on your lease: your local public school and how to enrol, your nearest GP, station and supermarket.

  2. 2

    Bin night, in your language. Which bin, which night, what goes where. In English, Hindi, Chinese, Arabic or Korean.

  3. 3

    Around you. Doctors, dentists, physio, pharmacy, shops, fuel, library, pool. Sorted by what you'll need first.

  4. 4

    Your first fortnight. Three things within walking distance in your first two weeks, and a New Neighbours Morning every month.

  5. 5

    Hot day. When the forecast turns hot, your nearest cool spaces move to the top.

For council

Council sees where. Never who.

Move forecast. Public bond data by postcode shows where people are leaving and arriving, so clean-up slots, drop-off days and welcomes go where they're needed.

A postcode and a week. No names, no addresses, no profiles.

Measured.

  • Dumping reports per 100 move-outs
  • Kilograms kept out of landfill
  • New arrivals who know a neighbour after three months

AI and data

What the AI does, and what it doesn't.

  • It suggests where each item goes. You decide.

  • It never reports you, and its answer isn't proof of anything.

  • ParraMate keeps no copy of your photo.

  • Every figure on this page links to its source. Sample data is labelled as sample.
